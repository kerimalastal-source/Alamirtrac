# Language policy

- Arabic (ar, RTL) is this site's native/primary language. All UI copy, content,
  and metadata are written in Arabic first.
- English is a secondary locale to be added later (not yet implemented) — do not
  remove or break the Arabic-first structure to make room for it prematurely.
- Every piece of Arabic text (UI strings, headings, body copy, metadata, form
  labels, error/success messages) must use correct, polished Modern Standard
  Arabic: proper hamza placement (أ إ ؤ ئ ء), correct تاء مربوطة/هاء endings,
  correct ال-definite article joining, natural word choice (avoid literal
  translation-style phrasing), and correct punctuation (، ؛ ؟). Prefer clear,
  professional, concise wording over stiff or awkward phrasing.
- When adding or editing any Arabic copy, proofread it against these standards
  before considering the change done.
